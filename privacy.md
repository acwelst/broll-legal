# B Roll privacy policy

*Last updated: 26 November 2026*

B Roll is a camera app made by Alexander Welst, in Victoria, Australia. This page says what the app does with your information. The short version: **your photos are yours, and we never see them.** There are no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs in the app.

## Your photos and videos

Photos, videos, Live moments, rolls and edits are kept on your iPhone, inside the app. B Roll only copies them somewhere else when you ask it to:

- **Save to Photos.** With "Save to Photos" on, or when you save or export something, a copy goes to your photo library, in a B Roll album.
- **Sharing.** When you share a photo or video, it goes wherever you send it.
- **Shared rolls.** See below.

We (the developer) never receive your photos or videos, except a small copy of a photo you choose to report (see "Reports and blocking").

## What B Roll asks permission for

- **Camera**: to take photos.
- **Microphone**: B Roll keeps a second or two of sound before each shot, so a photo comes with the moment that led up to it. The microphone is only used while the camera is open with Live moments on, and the sound is kept only with the photo it belongs to. Turn Live moments off and the microphone is switched off.
- **Photos**: to save your shots to your library and keep them together in a B Roll album.
- **Location (while using the app)**: to save in each photo where it was taken, so it shows on the map in Photos, and to name a roll's video after its place ("Amsterdam"). Apple's location service turns your position into a place name. You can switch location off in the viewfinder's menu, or deny the permission, and everything else keeps working. Location is **removed** from copies of photos added to a shared roll.

You can change any of these in the iPhone's Settings → B Roll.

## Shared rolls

A shared roll is a roll friends shoot into together. It is stored in the **iCloud account of the person who started it**, using Apple's CloudKit sharing, and uses their iCloud storage. Anyone who has the roll's link can join it while its host allows invites.

What other members of a shared roll see: the photos and Live moment clips added to it, the name you choose to show, and which camera took each photo and when. Not your location, Apple ID, email address or phone number.

We can't see shared rolls: they live in the host's iCloud, not on our servers. If you leave a roll, its photos are removed from your phone; photos other people have already downloaded stay on their phones. If the host deletes the roll, it is deleted for everyone.

## Reports

You can report a photo in a shared roll by tapping the name of the person who took it.

- **Reporting** removes the photo from your phone and opens an email to us with a small copy of the photo, the roll's and photo's IDs, and the names the reporter and the photo's taker use in the roll. We use it only to deal with the report, and delete it once the report is closed.
- **Removing and leaving.** The host can remove anyone from their roll, and you can leave any roll, which removes its photos from your phone.

## Anonymous usage counts

To learn which cameras people actually use and what to improve, B Roll counts what happens in the app: for example, photos taken per camera, flash on or off, lens and photo shape, rolls started, videos made, which settings are on and whether permissions were given. Once a day the app sends these counts to the app's database in Apple's CloudKit, along with:

- a random ID made by the app when it was installed (not linked to you, your Apple ID or your device's identifiers),
- the iPhone model, iOS version and app version,
- whether it's a TestFlight build.

The counts never include photos, places, roll names, the name you use in shared rolls, or anything you type. We can't tell who sent them, so we can't look up or delete one person's counts; turning counting off is the way to stop them.

**Turn it off** in Settings → System → "Share anonymous usage". Counts not yet sent are dropped. Usage counts are kept for at most two years.

## Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings, it's an ordinary email from your mail app: we receive your email address and what you write, plus the app version, iOS version and iPhone model the app adds to the message. We use it only to reply and fix things, and never add you to a mailing list.

## Purchases

If B Roll sells anything, Apple handles the payment. We never see your name, card or payment details: Apple tells the app only what you've bought, so it can unlock it.

## What we don't do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no selling or renting of data, no analytics, ad or crash-reporting companies, no accounts to sign up for.

## Children

B Roll isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anything from them.

## Your choices and rights

- Deleting a roll or the app deletes its photos from your phone (copies you saved to Photos stay there).
- You can switch off location, Live moments and usage counts at any time.
- Wherever you live (including under the Australian Privacy Act or the GDPR), you can ask what we hold about you or ask us to delete it. Apart from feedback emails and reports, there's nothing we can link to you. Contact us {% include contact.md %}.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If a change matters (for example, something new leaving your phone), the app will tell you.
