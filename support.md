# B Roll support

Something not working, or a camera you'd love to see? Get in touch {% include contact.md %}. Sending it from the app adds your app and iOS version, so we can help faster.

## Common questions

**Where are my photos?**
In the app, in their roll: tap the roll's name at the top of the viewfinder for the roll menu, or the photo stack to see them. Switch on Settings → Save to Photos to keep a copy in a B Roll album in Photos too.

**Why is the microphone on?**
B Roll keeps a second or two of sound before each shot, so a photo comes with the moment that led up to it. Turn Live moments off to take plain stills; the microphone switches off with them.

**How do I turn off location?**
In the viewfinder's menu, or in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → B Roll.

**How do shared rolls work?**
Open the roll menu, tap the invite button on a roll and send the link to friends. The roll lives in your iCloud and uses your iCloud storage. Anyone with the link can join while invites are open.

**Someone added a photo I don't want to see.**
Tap their name on the photo to report it or block them. If it's your roll, you can delete the photo for everyone or remove the person from the roll menu.

**Refunds**
Refunds go through Apple: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
